Massachusetts: use the official PAC finder

Inventing names would be worse than an empty list. Use Mass.gov.

Licensed Problem Animal Control (PAC) agents can be hired by landowners when a wild animal is damaging property or posing a safety threat. PAC agents are not the same as municipal animal-control officers, who mainly handle domestic animals. Paid problem-animal work in Massachusetts requires the PAC license.

Before you hire, read when to hire a licensed operator.
