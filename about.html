About Wildlife Control Index

Wildlife Control Index is an independent public directory compiled by Stephen Shortell. It lists people who already appear on official state license lists for commercial nuisance-wildlife work. It is a reading of those records, not a new credential and not a referral desk.

What this is not

  • Not a wildlife-control or pest-control company.
  • Not Shortell Designs, and not a product of any design studio.
  • Not endorsed by the Ohio Division of Wildlife, ODNR, NYSDEC, MassWildlife, USDA Wildlife Services, or any local health department.
  • Not a ranking or marketplace. There is no paid placement on the professional lists.
  • Not veterinary, public-health, or legal advice.

Where the names come from

Retrieved 2026-08-30.

  • Ohio (621 public listings). Transcribed from dow-list-cnwaco-current-license-holders.pdf, “Ohio Commercial Nuisance Wild Animal Control Operators Q2 2026,” updated 2026-06-30. Official PDF. ODNR notes the file is not exhaustive — only operators who asked to be listed. 603 Ohio addresses of record, 18 out of state.
  • New York (1204 currently licensed). Transcribed from NYSDEC’s Open Data NY dataset 868p-5nmh (1171 general, 33 municipality). Official dataset. All licenses in this extract expire 2026-09-30.
  • Massachusetts (0 names). MassWildlife’s PAC finder is a JavaScript map/list. Names were not present in the HTML. This index does not invent them.

Private directories (including WildlifeHelp.org) and trade-association rosters were not scraped.

Verify before you hire

  • Ohio: current CNWACO PDF from ODNR Division of Wildlife
  • New York: data.ny.gov 868p-5nmh and NYSDEC special licenses
  • Massachusetts: Mass.gov PAC finder
